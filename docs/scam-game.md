# Scam Games Reference — Flipped to Defense

Oct 24, 2026 · @Ryan

## Scam Together (Roblox)

[Scam Together](https://www.roblox.com/games/93539470484676/Scam-Together) is a call-center shift game: answer the phone, raise the caller's Trust meter through dialogue choices, take their money, and deposit enough before 5 PM to hit the daily quota. It launched in September 2026 and peaked at about 7,000 players in October, with 3.0M visits and a 78% like ratio ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10766502612/scam-together)). It's made by the group "DO NOT REDEEM IT" and runs on PC, mobile, tablet and console.

**One call, step by step** ([All Things How guide](https://allthings.how/scam-together-guide-best-classes-gems-and-fast-leveling-roblox/))

1. Desk phone rings; answer it.
2. Pick dialogue options from a panel. Good picks raise the **Trust meter**; slow or bad picks make the caller hang up and you get nothing.
3. When the caller agrees, press **Request Access** and take "Rewards Points" or "Gift Cards" from their screen. A **SCAMMED!** pop-up pays out.
4. Stall with more dialogue to keep them on the line and take more.
5. **Hang up on baiters**: fake callers who only waste your shift time.

**The shift**

- One shift = one in-game day. Day 1 on Easy has a **$500 quota**.
- Stolen cash counts only after you hit the green **DEPOSIT** floor switch. Pop-ups warn as 5 PM nears.
- A **Deposit Board** shows "DAILY QUOTA: $500 OF $500" and "GOAL REACHED."
- Settings before a shift: Max Players, Difficulty, office map. A limited **Scam O Ween** map runs for Halloween.

**Progression and monetization**

- **Scamazon** computer in the office sells upgrades ($250 each: Advanced Extractor, Smooth Talker, Stall Script) and office items at tier 10 (Lead Generator $450, Coffee Machine $250). An **Employees** tab lets you hire help.
- **Class Booth** rolls classes by rarity: Common 50%, Rare 39%, Epic 9.5%, Legendary 1.2% (Alien, The Closer, Trick-or-Treater), Mythic 0.3% (Kingpin). A pity counter guarantees Legendary or better at 100 spins.
- **Gems** come from daily login rewards (30 on day 1 up to 250 plus a Lucky Spin on day 7) or Robux (500 gems for 99 R$ up to 15,000 for 1,299 R$).
- The store page also promises team scams, betraying friends, quests, and "riskier scams pay more."

**What to borrow:** the Trust meter, the deposit-before-the-deadline tension, the fake-caller trap, and the physical office where everything (phone, store PC, deposit switch) is a thing you walk to.

## Scam With Your Friends (Steam)

[Scam With Your Friends](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4954910/Scam_With_Your_Friends/) is a 1 to 8 player co-op dark comedy where you talk out loud to AI-voiced callers, hack their PCs, and hit a rising daily quota or your boss fires the whole team. It entered Early Access on October 15, 2026 at $9.99 and is rated Very Positive (83% of 2,078 English reviews). Developers: JakeHub and Metater.

**One call, step by step** ([WindowsMode guide](https://www.windowsmode.com/?p=48107))

1. Pick a scam type: fake police fine, romance, lottery prize, utility bill.
2. Talk into your mic (or type). Speech recognition feeds an AI caller with its own voice and personality, so every call is improvised.
3. Watch the **trust meter** slide between trusting and angry. If they hang up, you get nothing.
4. Get a payment code, or a remote-access code to open their PC in a fake **AnyViewer** app and dig through their email for leverage.
5. Side bets: "coin investment" rounds that rise and crash at a random time; cash out early or lose it.

**The day**

- Each day unlocks new scams and raises the quota. Miss it and everyone is fired; the run restarts (run-based, permadeath).
- End-of-day **boss review** replays the funniest clips and ranks players by money made.
- Chaos between calls: **scambaiters** plant viruses on your PC, police raids, airstrikes. Players can throw props, tackle coworkers, and buy joke weapons or airstrikes from **Scamazon**, even aimed at their own team.
- Parody desktop apps: Scamazon, Disscord, AnyViewer, plus Paint, a webcam and a screen recorder ([wiki](https://scamwithyourfriends.wiki/)).
- A host-only **Family Friendly** mode tones down caller talk.

**What players say:** the humour comes from improvised conversations and friends talking over each other on one call ([dlcompare](https://www.dlcompare.com/gaming-news/scam-with-your-friends-turns-phone-scams-into-co-op-comedy-85830)). One commenter's criticism: co-op is mostly sitting in a chair talking to the AI, and misheard words confuse it.

**What to borrow:** a quota that escalates daily, team-wide failure (everyone is fired together), the boss review as a funny scoreboard, and office chaos events that pull players off the phones.

## Why they're fun

Both games run the same engine: a short conversation you can win or lose, a meter that shows how it's going, a daily money target, and chaos that interrupts you. The Roblox version swaps live AI voice for dialogue choices, which is the version you can build in 8 hours.

| Mechanic | Scam Together (Roblox) | Scam With Your Friends (Steam) | Keep for your game? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conversation | Pick dialogue options | Talk live to AI callers | Yes, as dialogue choices |
| Feedback meter | Trust meter | Trust meter, trusting to angry | Yes, flipped to Suspicion |
| Daily target | $500 quota, deposit by 5 PM | Rising quota, boss review | Yes, a damage budget per day |
| Failure | Quota missed | Whole team fired, run restarts | Yes, team-wide |
| Trap callers | Baiters waste your time | Scambaiters plant viruses | Yes: real customers who look suspicious |
| Interruptions | Shift timer pressure | Viruses, raids, airstrikes | Yes, office events |
| Office as a map | Phone, store PC, deposit switch | Desk PCs, props to throw | Yes, stations to run between |
| Upgrades | Scamazon gear, hired employees | Scamazon joke items, unlocks | Light: 2 or 3 upgrades |
| Gacha classes | Class Booth by rarity | None | Skip for the hackathon |
| Boss review | Deposit Board | Clip replay and ranking | Yes, an end-of-day report |

## The flip: a defender game

Your team staffs a company's front office during a scam wave; every call, email and payment request is a ticket, and your job is to let real business through while blocking the scams. It keeps everything that makes both scam games fun (a timed shift, a meter, a quota, team-wide failure) but players win by spotting tells instead of exploiting them. Working names that pitch it on sight: *Scam Stoppers Inc.*, *Don't Pay That*, *Fraud Desk*.

```text
Core loop: one ticket, three outcomes
Ticket arrives -> Inspect the tells -> Your call?
   Your call? --verify--> Verify by callback (~5 s) --reveals truth--> Correct call
   Your call? --approved a scam--> Scam approved: company loses the money
   Your call? --rejected a real one--> Real request rejected: small penalty
   Your call? --right call--> Correct call: money saved
All outcomes -> End-of-day report (saved vs. lost, boss review) -> next shift, harder -> Ticket arrives
```

Verifying is always safe but slow, so the tension is how many tickets you can afford to check before the queue overflows.

**Mapping the originals onto the defender**

| In the scam games | In your game |
| --- | --- |
| Raise the victim's Trust meter | Watch a **Suspicion meter** rise as you spot tells |
| Hit a money quota | Protect a **Company Budget**; every approved scam drains it |
| Baiters waste your time | **Real customers who look suspicious**: rejecting them costs you |
| Deposit before 5 PM | Clear the queue before 5 PM; unanswered tickets count against you |
| Boss review | End-of-day report: saved, lost, best catch, worst miss |
| Scamazon upgrades | Security upgrades: spam filter, caller ID, a second callback phone |

**Roles for 2 to 4 players** (anyone can do any station; roles just split attention)

| Role | Station | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front desk | Ringing phone | Takes calls, reads the dialogue, flags tells |
| Inbox | PC | Checks sender addresses and links, reports phishing |
| Finance | Payment desk | Must approve every payment; big ones need a **second player's sign-off** (dual approval) |
| IT | Callback phone, server rack | Verifies by calling known numbers, cleans infected PCs |

**Win and lose**

- Survive a shift with the Budget above zero; each new day adds tickets and subtler tells.
- Budget hits zero and the company goes under: everyone is fired together (the Steam game's best tension).
- Stretch mode, **The Insider**: one player is secretly working for the scammers and tries to approve bad tickets without getting caught. The team votes them out at the end of the shift. That borrows Murder Mystery 2's social deduction, the #2 game on Roblox right now.

## Scam content to use

Build every scam the scammers throw at your company from real, documented tactics, so the game teaches real skills. Business email compromise alone cost US businesses $3.05 billion across 24,768 complaints in 2025, the second-biggest loss category in the FBI's report ([McDonald Hopkins summary of IC3 2025](https://www.mcdonaldhopkins.com/insights/news/the-sobering-truth-of-the-fbis-2025-internet-crime-complaint-center-report)).

| Scam (in-game ticket) | What arrives at your desk | Tells to plant | Correct counter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fake CEO | Email or call from "the CEO": wire $40k now, keep it quiet | Urgency + secrecy, slightly wrong address, won't confirm by phone | Call back the CEO's known number |
| Vendor invoice change | Supplier says "we changed banks, pay this new account" | New account number, amount or timing off, changed reply-to | Verify with the vendor's number on file; dual approval |
| Payroll diversion | "Employee" asks to switch their direct deposit | Request by email only, new bank, urgency before payday | Confirm in person or through HR's known contact |
| Gift card request | Boss or "IT" asks someone to buy gift cards and send the codes | Gift cards as payment, specific stores, "send the PIN" | Always reject: no real business asks for this ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/node/328341)) |
| Tech support | Caller says your PC is infected, needs remote access | Unsolicited contact, asks for a connection code | Hang up, report to your own IT |
| Phishing email | "Reset your password" or an unexpected invoice attachment | Look-alike domain, generic greeting, link text ≠ real URL, bad grammar, urgent attachment ([CISA](https://files.nyit.edu/files/its/CISA_Avoiding_Social_Engineering_and_Phishing_Attacks.pdf)) | Report, don't click; check the real URL |
| Info pretexting | Caller asks who handles payroll or what software you use | Unsolicited questions about staff or systems | Don't share; verify identity with the company |
| Government threat | "IRS" or "police" demands an immediate fine | Threats, deadlines, unusual payment method | Reject; agencies don't demand payment this way |

Red flags come from [CISA](https://files.nyit.edu/files/its/CISA_Avoiding_Social_Engineering_and_Phishing_Attacks.pdf), the [FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/node/328341) and [Huntington's BEC guide](https://www.huntington.com/Commercial/insights/business-email-compromise). The rule that ties them together is the game's core lesson: **verify through a channel you already trust, never the one the request came from.**

**Legit tickets matter as much as scams.** Mix in real requests that share some tells (a rushed but genuine CEO, a vendor that really did change banks and confirms on callback). If every urgent request is a scam, players stop thinking. Rejecting a real request should also cost you.

## 8-hour scope

Ship one office, one shift, four ticket types and three decisions; everything else is a stretch goal.

**Must have (hours 0.5 to 5)**

- [ ] One office map with 3 stations: Phone desk, Inbox PC, Finance desk with a callback phone
- [ ] Ticket system on the server: random tickets every few seconds, each with a type, a legit/scam flag, an amount, and 1 to 3 planted tells
- [ ] Ticket panel UI: sender, message, amount, and three buttons: Approve, Verify, Reject
- [ ] Verify = walk to the callback phone, wait about 5 seconds, get the truth (costs time, not money)
- [ ] Company Budget bar: drops on every approved scam; small penalty for rejecting a legit ticket
- [ ] 4-minute shift with an end-of-day report: money saved, money lost, scams caught
- [ ] 4 ticket types: Fake CEO, Vendor bank change, Gift card request, Phishing link

**Should have (hours 5 to 7, the feel pass)**

- [ ] Dual approval: payments over a threshold need two players to press Approve
- [ ] Big red flash, alarm and "-$40,000" floater when a scam gets through; green "SCAM BLOCKED" with confetti when caught
- [ ] Ringing phones, inbox ding, a wall clock counting to 5 PM
- [ ] Difficulty ramp: more tickets and subtler tells as the shift goes on

**Stretch (only if ahead)**

- [ ] The Insider mode: one secret scammer on the team
- [ ] Office events (virus, power cut)
- [ ] Tutorial ticket on the first shift that points out each tell

**Cut entirely:** live AI voice calls, gacha classes, Robux shop, saving progress between sessions.

**Plan order with your setup:** have agy plan the ticket system first (`plans/tickets.md`: ticket data in `src/shared/`, generation and validation in `src/server/Systems/`, panel in `src/client/Controllers/`), then the office map, then the end-of-day report. The server decides whether a ticket is a scam; the client only shows it and sends the button press.
